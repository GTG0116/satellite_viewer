Display

3D cloud volume

Opens a 3D view beside the map (below it on phones). Draw a box or an area (20–1500 km) over the clouds: they are rebuilt as volumetric clouds from the satellite bands (cloud-top height, optical depth, bases by cloud type) over real terrain. Drag the corners to reshape, the green dot to move the 3D camera.

3D on the map

Desktop: right-drag or Ctrl-drag to tilt / rotate. Touch: two-finger twist rotates, two-finger slide up / down tilts.

About

Imagery is decoded in your browser from the raw NOAA Open Data Dissemination files (GOES ABI NetCDF‑4 / HDF5, Himawari HSD bzip2, GK2A AMI NetCDF‑4). Map: MapLibre GL JS, OpenFreeMap / OpenStreetMap; terrain: AWS Terrain Tiles; 3D ground imagery: Esri; city lights: NASA GIBS. Nearest-pixel display, no smoothing.
